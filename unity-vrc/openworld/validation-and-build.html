Validation and build options

Open Tools > OpenWorld > Validation, or click Validate on OpenWorld Settings. A VRChat build stops if there are errors. Warnings don't stop it.

Budgets​

Budgets are checked on the busiest window of chunks within the mid rings, with all of it assumed to be in view.

BudgetPCQuest
Draw calls400150
Triangles1,000,000150,000
Resident streamed textures512 MB128 MB
Suggested maximum mid rings32

Going over a budget is a warning. Blocks count as one draw per 1023 instances of each type, and impostor pages are added. Terrains, Dig Zones, entities and avatars aren't counted. These are starting values that haven't been profiled on Quest hardware.

Errors​

Message is aboutFix
Empty gridSet Chunk Size, Chunks X and Chunks Z.
Chunk roots don't match the gridClick Assign To Chunks.
Near rings exceed mid ringsLower the near rings or raise the mid rings.
Merge Rings × Block Chunks + 1Raise it above the mid rings.
Object inside the grid but not in a chunk rootClick Assign To Chunks.
Block Prefabs set but nothing baked, or a stale Block layoutClick Assign To Chunks.
A Block type lost its mesh or materialClick Assign To Chunks.
Material has GPU Instancing offTurn it on. Its Blocks don't draw without it.
Chunk colliders not bakedClick Assign To Chunks.
Editable world without an edit manager per regionClick Assign To Chunks.

Warnings​

WarningWhat to do
A renderer is wider than two chunksSplit it. It appears and disappears with only one chunk.
Quest or PC mid rings above the suggested valueLower them, or profile.
Quest applies more than 1 chunk change per frameSet it to 1 to avoid hitches.
No impostors bakedClick Bake Impostors. Far chunks show nothing without them.
Too many draw callsMerge meshes, use Blocks, or lower the mid rings.
Too many trianglesUse lighter meshes or lower the mid rings.
Too much streamed texture memorySet Load Distance on OpenWorldStream, or ship smaller images.

Build options​

On OpenWorld Settings:

OptionEffectTrade-off
Bake On BuildBefore each VRChat build, re-assigns chunks, re-reads terrain trees, bakes impostors and saves the sceneLonger builds
Strip Detail MapsRemoves normal, metallic/specular, occlusion, height and detail maps from scene materials and renders them flatSmaller download, flatter look
No Static BatchingTurns static batching offMuch smaller download, more draw calls
Unbatch Repeated MeshesLeaves a mesh out of static batching when at least this many renderers share it (0 = off)Each such mesh ships once. Enable GPU Instancing on its materials.

The material options work on copies, so your own materials don't change.

Chunk gizmos​

OptionShows
Show Chunk BoxesA box around each chunk's content. In Play mode: green is near, yellow is mid, grey is off.
Show Chunk DetailsName, Blocks, terrain trees and colliders per chunk, while the world is selected
Details DistanceHow close to the Scene camera labels are drawn
Show Chunk BordersBorders around the chunk the Scene camera is in
